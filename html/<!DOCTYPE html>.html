CSS Box Styling Comparison

Solid Border

Borders follow the border-radius.

border: 5px solid #2ecc71;
Outline + Offset

Outlines do NOT follow border-radius and can be spaced out.

outline-offset: 10px;
Double Style

Creates two parallel lines within the border width.

border-style: double;
Groove + Outline

Combining a 3D border with a tight solid outline.

border-style: groove;
